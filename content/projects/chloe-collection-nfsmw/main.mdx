An overhaul mod for Need for Speed: Most Wanted with many new features and improvements.

# Disclaimer

Due to the current programming landscape, I feel that it's necessary to explicitly state that this project had zero assistance or any other kind of involvement from any sort of "AI agent" and it never will.  
This mod was entirely built by hand, by a human being, and I believe that any project that cannot also claim this about itself is not worth people's time. The only acceptable amount of AI use is zero AI use.

# Installation

- Make sure you have v1.3 of the game, as this is the only version this plugin is compatible with. (exe size of 6029312 bytes)
- Plop the files into your game folder.
- Start the game, create a new savegame and start the career.
- If the game doesn't launch or has performance issues, try deleting d3d9.dll.
- Enjoy, nya~ :3

# Features

- Added basic support for crew members - Mia or Rog are now able to win races for you if they're present in an event
- All cars and upgrades are unlocked from the start and their prices adjusted, adding a new layer of strategy into balancing your spending
- All rivals' pursuit milestones have been changed, with some brand new types of milestones added such as immobilizing police vehicles or traveling a certain distance
- Blacklist members no longer have a quota of races and milestones to complete, instead relying on a global counter of Rep points for both
- Boss skip has been implemented as a feature to improve the game's pacing, Taz, Big Lou, Kaze and Webster have been removed
- Cars lost by pink slip are kept track of and used by opponents in the late game
- Circuit races have been toned down, with most of them having reduced lap counts
- Circuit races with 1 lap now show a completion percentage rather than lap 1/1
- Collectibles have been added around the map in the form of graffiti, spraying over them grants you a discount on cars & upgrades
- Collisions are now always loaded across the entire map, thereby reducing rubberbanding and making sure that the world can never unload and become non-solid
- Cops can now get destroyed if they crash into roadblocks
- Cops in the final pursuit are now a lot more difficult
- Cops now have a higher chance to show up in a race (including in a rival challenge) if you're driving a car with a higher heat level, per-race cop probability has been removed
- Cops on heat levels 1 and 2 no longer stop spawning after the first cop is destroyed, thereby making them actually viable for working on milestones
- EA Trax is now set to shuffle by default
- Ending a rival challenge with the cops on you now means you need to escape before continuing
- Finishing the game now unlocks heat level 6 and enables the ability to redo milestones
- Fixed a softlock in the beginning where the safehouse was not accessible
- Fixed numerous grammatical errors and issues in the subtitles for SMS messages and FMVs
- Fixed Razor's challenge text being bugged and not showing up immediately
- Fixed some pursuit breakers not having icons on the minimap
- Freeroam cops are no longer present unless your car is heat level 3 or higher
- Game Moment Camera is now disabled by default
- Jewels now drives a much more upgraded Mustang GT for balancing reasons
- JV now drives a Corvette C6 for balancing reasons
- Marker selection has been entirely removed, each rival after Sonny now gives you their pink slip by default, and some of them also give you an extra Junkman marker or cash reward
- Most rivals now have a Special Challenge in the Milestones menu, requiring you to complete a pursuit with various gimmicks or prerequisites
- Opponents are now more difficult (based on the Hard difficult in Quick Race, so it's still vanilla-like) and they instantly fire their NOS after the starting countdown
- Pink slip cars now come with heat levels higher than 1x
- Racers can now show up as finished on the leaderboard
- Razor now drives an M3 GTR with Ultimate NOS for balancing reasons
- Removed samey and repeated events in the rivals' normal races and their rival challenges
- Rival challenge intro cutscenes are now skippable
- Rival challenges after Sonny now have your car on the line as well as theirs, you can lose your car if you lose against them
- Rival challenges are now way more difficult, with the opponents driving as fast as they should given the cars they have
- Rog is now a full Blacklist member, complete with his own objectives
- Ronnie now drives a fully upgraded DB9 for balancing reasons
- Some races now have unique characters as opponents, with their own unique cars just like in ProStreet
- Some rival challenges now start by playing specific songs on the soundtrack
- Speedtrap milestones have been removed
- The cash reward for having an Underground 2 save has been removed in favor of getting cash from Rog's races
- The entire map in freeroam is now unlocked from the start
- The first few Blacklist rivals now award you with special race events from other Need For Speed titles, accessible through the Bonus Races menu
- The game now autosaves way more often, negating the effect of crashes (which there are hopefully very few of)
- The lane system has been removed from Drag races
- The restart button after race wins is no longer usable, in order to prevent you from bypassing post-race pursuits
- Tollbooth races now have you racing against a ghost of each rival, representing their time for that event
- Unused SMS messages have been restored, e.g. Vic now calls the cops on you
- Your car's heat level now only increases from taking down cops, no longer passively going up over time during a chase
- ([Part of CwoeeQOL](https://codeberg.org/gaycoderprincess/MostWantedCwoeeQOL/releases/latest)) Borderless Windowed is now toggleable in the Video menu
- ([Part of CwoeeQOL](https://codeberg.org/gaycoderprincess/MostWantedCwoeeQOL/releases/latest)) Motion Blur is now toggleable in the Video menu
- ([Part of CwoeeQOL](https://codeberg.org/gaycoderprincess/MostWantedCwoeeQOL/releases/latest)) Subtitles are now toggleable in the Audio menu
- ([Part of Open Limit Adjuster](https://codeberg.org/gaycoderprincess/MostWantedOpenLimitAdjuster/releases/latest)) Racers now no longer despawn after the race ends, permanently staying on the map through post-race pursuits
- ([Part of Open Limit Adjuster](https://codeberg.org/gaycoderprincess/MostWantedOpenLimitAdjuster/releases/latest)) Traffic now has the same spawn rate in pursuits as it does in freeroam

# Achievements

- Busywork - Spray over all tags
- Challenge Complete - Win a Special Challenge
- Completionist - Reach 100% game completion
- Masochistic Tendencies - Beat Razor in a Lexus IS300
- Most Wanted - Finish the game
- Multitasking - Spray over a tag while racing
- Overachiever - Finish all of a rival's objectives
- Own The City - Win all races
- Public Enemy - Complete all milestones
- Risking It All - Win a pink slip challenge
- Starting Point - Win a race
- World Traveler - Win all bonus races
